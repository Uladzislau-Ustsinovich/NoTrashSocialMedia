# NoTrash privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

NoTrash is a browser for social media sites that hides parts of their pages. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app's developer receives nothing.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. NoTrash has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. The app never sends anything to its developer.

## What stays on your device

- **Your settings**: which filters are on, and your focus settings (pause before opening, daily limits, grayscale).
- **Your usage time**: how long each site was open in the app, per day, for the last 14 days. It is used only to show you your own time and to apply the limits you set.
- **Website data**: cookies and other data that the sites you open store in the app's built-in browser, which is how you stay signed in.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

## The sites you open

NoTrash loads the sites you choose (for example Instagram or X) directly from their owners. When you use them, you deal with those companies exactly as you would in any browser: they receive what you send them, set their own cookies, and apply their own privacy policies. You sign in on their pages; NoTrash never sees, stores or transmits your passwords.

NoTrash changes how those pages look on your device by hiding parts of them. It does not read, copy or send out their content or your activity on them.

NoTrash is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram, Meta, X Corp. or any other site it can open.

## Children

NoTrash is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
